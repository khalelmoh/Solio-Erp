# Financial lifecycle extension — design and policies

The first milestone was reverified before extension: 30 automated tests and the real browser workflow passed, including the $50,000/$40,000/$10,000/$6,000/$4,000 scenario. Existing database records are migrated in place without reset. The stack remains Node.js, SQLite, browser JavaScript and Playwright.

## Changes and invariants

- Budget increases/reductions remain complete immutable versions. A dedicated transfer operation builds a new version from two existing category/period lines. No proposal affects authority before independent approval. Approval checks existing actuals and reservations; the existing explicit finance overrun authority remains the only permitted budget exception. Supporting files are retained against the proposal and approvers remain in immutable history.
- Advance requests record beneficiary, employee/supplier type and due date before approval, using the configured spending approval policy. Approved advance requests reserve budget once, in the same commitment ledger with an advance classification. Disbursement changes cash/advance asset only. Accepted expenditure charges cost and releases its reservation; unused cash returns release their reservation automatically. Legacy generic requests may still be disbursed as advances, with explicitly recorded beneficiary and due date. Overdue status derives from due date and outstanding amount; no notification delivery is inferred.
- Evidence is stored in the database with filename, MIME type, byte size and SHA-256. Downloads use attachment disposition, role checks and a MIME whitelist. PDF, PNG, JPEG and plain text are supported, bounded to 512 KiB/file and four files/operation. Settlements of new advance requests require receipts. No external file path is accepted.
- Statement headers and imported/entered lines are immutable. CSV format is `external_id,date,description,amount`; positive amounts are receipts and negative amounts are cash outflows. Opening + movements must equal closing. Account/date ranges may not overlap and external IDs cannot be reused for the same account. Matching is an append-only many-to-many allocation with checks on both statement and book remainders under the database write lock. Matching creates no financial journal or expense. Unmatching needs a reason; finalized statements must be reopened with an audit reason first.
- Finalization requires every statement line matched, zero book/statement/outstanding difference and explicit reasons for outstanding book entries. Full statement matching, not legacy balance-only reconciliation, supports new financial closeout. Linked book correction pairs that did not occur at the bank can be reviewed and offset explicitly; they are never fabricated as statement transactions.
- Credit notes debit payable up to each allocation's unpaid liability and put any excess in a supplier-refund receivable; credit the original expense/asset and reverse its budget charge. Default treatment cancels the cost without recreating a commitment. An explicit replacement-goods choice restores the commitment and is budget checked. Refund receipts debit cash and credit that receivable without a second cost change.
- Posted records remain immutable. Corrections use linked reversal journals. Advance, credit and refund reversals validate downstream dependencies. A correction to an original locked period requires independent administrator authorization for the named finance poster, original journal, expiry and reason; the correction must be posted in an open period. Locked periods cannot be reopened through the ordinary period update API.
- Operational completion stops new spending requests/POs/advance disbursements but allows existing obligations to be settled. Financial closure uses an itemized closeout checklist. Unresolved commitments, bills, supplier credits, advances, cash matching, restricted cash and outstanding promises block closure. An independent administrator may authorize specified balances to remain, documenting owner and treatment against a snapshot; closure does not pretend those balances were cleared. Reopening requires administrator authority and a reason, recorded in audit/history.
- Source returns reduce cash and funding control. Reports show received funding and returned funding separately; cancelling an unreceived promise is an auditable noncash adjustment.

## Reporting conventions

Reports filter a project, source, category and date range. Budget is authority for overlapping budget periods from the latest approved version as of the report end; actuals and reservations are cumulative through the end. Separate period movements avoid mixing flows with closing balances. Funding/cash have source dimensions but no category dimension: category-filtered reports label them as source/project totals, never invent a category allocation. Payments, liabilities and advance assets can use their request allocation categories. Funding outstanding is the sum of max(promised minus received, zero) by source. Advance reservations are included in total commitments and shown separately. CSV exports preserve exact decimal strings and neutralize spreadsheet formula text. Printable reports use the browser's print dialog.

## Policies still requiring organization configuration

Approval thresholds/roles; overrun limits and expiry; advance deadlines and permitted evidence; accounting calendar; administrator correction/closeout authority; reasons for outstanding reconciliation items; supplier replacement-versus-cancellation treatment; residual donor funds and unreceived award cancellation; retention and backup/security policies. No statutory payroll or integration defaults are introduced. The application remains a local pilot, not production-ready.
