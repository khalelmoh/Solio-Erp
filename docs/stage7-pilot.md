# Controlled-pilot preparation

Prepared 2026-10-19. **Synthetic staging only is authorized. A pilot containing real records is not authorized.** Keep the guided user outcome scoped to its completed financial scenario. See [readiness register](stage7-readiness.md).

Proposed later real pilot: one separately approved project, one reviewed USD funding source and five employees over ten working days. Actual project, source, participants and dates are **unassigned**. Do not convert the synthetic UAT project into real financial history or use demo accounts/passwords. Monthly/open/project pay only; no hourly base pay.

The synthetic Manager/Finance/Requester identities used in UAT are role accounts, not these named organization owners. Missing names are not replaced by fictional people.

Before introduction of real data, freeze the legacy cutoff and approve supported source imports/openings. Reconcile each supplier/employee liability, advance, project/source cash allocation and budget commitment to the reviewed trial balance and bank evidence. No double-counted opening expense or reservation. Opening warehouse lots and pre-depreciated legacy assets remain unsupported; implement and validate required imports separately. Configure actual authority thresholds, period locks, donor restrictions/residual treatments, benefits/tax/leave/final-pay rules, asset settings, retention/holds and backup policy. Existing illustrative statutory/retention settings are not validation.

During a future authorized pilot, Finance reconciles daily: posted source rows vs project/dashboard/CSV and trial balance; bank/mobile-money statements vs matched cash; bills vs partial payments; advances issued/accepted/returned vs receivables/reservations; payroll gross/net/recovery/benefit liabilities vs source allocation. HR reviews approvals, leave, bank changes and revoked access. The manager reviews available budget, restrictions and unbilled commitments. The administrator checks health/jobs, backup freshness and alerts. Every issue records time, project/source IDs, role, expected/actual result, evidence, severity, owner and resolution; protect private employee information.

Success criteria: zero unexplained monetary differences, duplicate postings or unauthorized disclosure; correct drill-down/export and approval history; accurate outstanding balances; tested offboarding; successful target recovery and accepted measured response times; actual reviewer findings for all remaining acceptance scenarios. Scope expansion requires the designated author's explicit decision after the reviews.

Stop new posting and outbound work on an unexplained balance, duplicate, privacy breach, lost attachment, failed recovery, unavailable authorized approval or failed/stale backup without an approved treatment. Preserve source records/logs and capture a fresh protected snapshot; do not erase evidence or relax controls to continue. Finance and the recovery owner reconcile all later external events before restoration/promotion. Prefer a forward fix; never overwrite newer financial records with an older snapshot. The [target recovery procedure](stage7-staging.md) and earlier [release/rollback procedure](stage6-operations.md) apply.
