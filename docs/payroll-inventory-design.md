# Payroll and inventory extension

The user requires USD only and pay independent of hours. Employee compensation supports monthly salary, an open amount for each run, or explicit project-based pay. Monthly terms optionally prorate by calendar days employed, excluding independently approved unpaid leave. Cash allowances specify taxable status, employee deductions reduce net pay, and employer benefits increase project cost. Fixed components optionally follow salary proration. Effective terms and component changes require independent payroll approval; each run freezes encrypted calculations and evidence. Payroll approval/posting reject changed terms or leave evidence and approval of one's own pay. Existing monthly employees and posted payroll remain intact.

Tax brackets and employer statutory rates remain organization-verified configuration. GL 2110 is the existing remittance control for tax, contributions, employee deductions and employer benefits; payslips/registers show their separate components. Net and remittance vouchers discharge those controls without charging budget again. No hourly wage calculation or statutory filing submission is included.

Warehouses belong to projects. Inventory retains original project/funding-source ownership and receipt lots. Quantity uses three decimal places; value uses USD cents. Finance submits and posts documents; independent project approval follows the existing journal policy. Receipts link an asset-classified supplier bill allocation and cannot exceed its net unreceived value. The invoice already charges budget and creates the liability. Receipt reclassifies its asset value into GL 1400; issue recognizes expense without charging purchase budget twice. Warehouse transfers preserve lot ownership and value. Partial issues/returns consume proportional remaining lot carrying value, rounding down to cents and consuming all cents on final depletion.

Count adjustments freeze expected quantity/value and require approval. Gains/losses post to GL 5300, separately from purchase-budget charges. A positive count uses the original receipt unit value. Supplier returns move value back to the original asset account; the linked supplier credit then reduces liability/budget, and any refund follows the existing receivable workflow. Credits cannot remove value still received into stock or already issued. Reverse dependent stock movements before receipts, and reverse/reduce receipts before reversing their supplier bill. Posted inventory documents are corrected through linked reversals, including accounting-period authorization.

Negative stock, cross-project warehouse transfers, backdated lot movements and stale approval snapshots are rejected inside the existing serialized transaction. Project closure checks residual stock and pending inventory documents. Project inventory reports provide source journals, lots, quantities, values and CSV exports; payroll registers stay HR/payroll restricted. Procurement receipt-before-invoice accruals, serial/batch expiration tracking, manufacturing and jurisdiction-specific filings are excluded from this milestone.
